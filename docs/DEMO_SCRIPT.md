# Demo script

About four minutes. Do not sign unless you mean to send a real order. Do not claim PnL.

| Time | Where | Say | Data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | `/` | Execution intelligence for tokenized equities on BSC. Discover, assess, policy, passport, receipt. | Live session if the desk loaded. Blank fields stay blank. |
| 0:30 | `/markets` | One cash name, three wrappers. Open the ticker. | Live quotes. |
| 1:00 | `/opportunities` | Gross gap is not net edge. Fees and slippage stay separate. Policy can block. | Live scan. |
| 1:30 | `/desk` | Passport, policy verdict, simulate for SWAP. Stop before confirm if you are not sending. | Live quote. Not a fill. |
| 2:15 | `/agents` | Agentic Wallet status, Studio ping, MCP tools that do not sign. | Say NOT CONNECTED if the ping or wallet is down. |
| 2:45 | `/activity` | Stored passport, commitment, receipt. Empty is an honest empty. | Live store. Not replay. |
| 3:15 | `/developer` | Observed Binance calls. Rates and p95 only after enough samples. | Live devex log. |
| 3:45 | `/replay` | Optional. Pick low liquidity or sim-ok. Play until the stop line. | Replay. No live transaction. |

Avoid the confirm button, broadcast, and RFQ submit unless a real send is intended.

Command palette: Replay a Scenario. Replay is not in the sidebar.
